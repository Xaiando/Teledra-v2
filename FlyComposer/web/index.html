What this experiment is testing

The connection graph comes from a published male fruit-fly connectome. Neuron dynamics, musical interpretation and feedback rules are choices made for this experiment. This is not a claim that a fly brain composes or hears music. The shuffled network helps test whether the particular wiring contributes anything worth hearing.

Fly Composer is a standalone candidate for Buzzart. Replacing the court’s Organist depends on the music earning that place through listening.
